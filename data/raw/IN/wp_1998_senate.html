The 1998 United States Senate election in Indiana was held November 3, 1998. Republican incumbent Dan Coats decided to retire instead of seeking a second full term in office. Democratic former governor Evan Bayh won the open seat his father once held.

Coats was later returned to the seat in the 2010 election.

General election

[edit]

Candidates

[edit]
  • Evan Bayh, former governor of Indiana (Democratic)
  • Paul Helmke, mayor of Fort Wayne (Republican)
  • Rebecca Sink-Burris (Libertarian)
